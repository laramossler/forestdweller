Fall in the Pacific Northwest means mushroom season. This year, the property produced an abundance of golden chanterelles—enough to eat fresh, dry for winter, and share with friends.

## Foraging Notes

The chanterelles appeared in late October after the first heavy rains. They favor the mossy areas under old-growth Douglas fir, exactly where we've been working to clear invasive species.

Key identification features:
- Golden to orange color
- False gills (ridges, not true gills)
- Fruity, apricot-like smell
- White flesh throughout

We harvested carefully, cutting with a knife and leaving the base to continue producing. Never take more than half of what you find.

## The Recipe

**Wild Mushroom Risotto** (serves 4)

Ingredients:
- 2 cups chanterelles, cleaned and roughly chopped
- 1.5 cups arborio rice
- 6 cups vegetable or chicken stock, kept warm
- 1 small onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 1/2 cup parmesan, grated
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- Fresh thyme
- Salt and pepper

Instructions:

1. Sauté chanterelles in 1 tablespoon butter until they release their moisture and begin to brown. Set aside.

2. In a heavy-bottomed pot, heat olive oil and 1 tablespoon butter. Sauté onion until soft, add garlic and thyme.

3. Add rice, stirring to coat with oil. Toast for 2 minutes.

4. Add wine, stirring until absorbed.

5. Add stock one ladle at a time, stirring frequently. Wait until each addition is mostly absorbed before adding more. This takes 20-25 minutes.

6. When rice is creamy but still has slight bite, stir in chanterelles, remaining butter, and parmesan.

7. Season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately.

## Notes

The key to risotto is patience and attention. You cannot rush it. You cannot walk away from it. This is cooking as meditation.

The chanterelles add an earthy, slightly peppery flavor that pairs perfectly with the creamy rice. This is food that tastes like a specific place and time—fall in the Pacific Northwest, rain on the forest floor, golden mushrooms in the moss.
